How Hookback handles your data

Effective 6 September 2026. Hookback works without an account.

Data stored on your device

Your clips, loop points, answers, scores, and practice history stay in this browser’s IndexedDB.

Microphone audio is analyzed in memory. Hookback stores its pitch track, then discards the recording.

Hookback does not upload clip or microphone audio.

License checks and payments

A Studio license token and its latest result stay in localStorage.

Hookback sends only that token to the Sociobot billing API for verification.

Sociobot and Dodo handle checkout as merchant of record. Hookback never receives card details.

Network requests

Hookback has no ads, behavioral analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party fonts and scripts.

The host may keep short server access logs for security and page counts.

Your controls

Export a JSON backup from the footer. The backup includes your audio, so store it carefully.

Remove clips from the queue. Clear this site’s browser data to remove all local Hookback data.

Contact

Send privacy questions through Sociobot (external site).

Loop short song phrases and compare your answer on this device.
